Frequently Asked Procurement Questions

Can we claim 100% GST input tax credit on purchases and rentals?

Yes, absolutely. We issue a formal Tax Invoice carrying your company's 15-digit GSTIN number for all hardware purchases, component orders, and monthly rental contracts. Your accounting team can claim 18% GST input credit seamlessly on GSTR-2B.

What happens if a rental laptop breaks down or develops a fault?

Our B2B rental contracts include an ironclad 24 to 48-hour hardware replacement SLA. If a laptop screen, motherboard, or keyboard fails during working hours, our technician will visit your office with an equivalent standby machine to swap it immediately so your employee faces zero downtime.

Are the refurbished laptops genuinely Grade A+ enterprise machines?

We deal exclusively in tier-1 commercial series laptops (ThinkPad, Dell Latitude, HP EliteBook) sourced directly from corporate refresh cycles. Unlike plastic consumer laptops, these feature magnesium-alloy roll cages, spill-resistant keyboards, and military-grade durability. Each unit undergoes our 30-point diagnostics before dispatch.

Can we test sample machines before placing a 50+ unit order?

Yes! For orders of 15 units or more, we deliver 1 or 2 evaluation units to your office location for a 3-day testing window. Your IT team can install custom company images, check performance metrics, and test battery life before signing off on the bulk fleet contract.
